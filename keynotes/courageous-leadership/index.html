Signature Keynote

Courageous Leadership

The hardest part of leadership isn't strategy. It's courage — the hard conversation, the unpopular call, the stand that costs something.

The Talk

What your audience walks away with.

Most leaders know what the right thing is. Far fewer do it when it's inconvenient. Shannon shows audiences how to build moral courage on purpose — so values become action, not a poster on the wall — and so the hardest calls get made when they need to be.

Who It's For

Leadership development programs and offsites
