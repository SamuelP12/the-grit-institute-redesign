Meet shannon Polson

One of the first women to fly the Apache helicopter in the U.S. Army, Shannon Huffman Polson has led from the cockpit to the corporate boardroom.

After leading attack helicopter line units on three continents, at Microsoft and in the non-profit sector, she founded The Grit Institute to help leaders thrive through purpose, resilience, and courage.

She’s the author of The Grit Factor and The Power of Purpose (forthcoming), and a sought-after keynote speaker for organizations around the world.
