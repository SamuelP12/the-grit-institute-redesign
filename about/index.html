Meet shannon Polson

Shannon Huffman Polson is a pioneer in leadership, grit, and purpose-driven impact. One of the first women to fly the Apache helicopter in the U.S. Army, she has led in the cockpit, the corporate boardroom, and on the global stage as a keynote speaker and author.

Through The Grit Institute, Shannon empowers leaders and organizations to reconnect to what matters most, strengthen resilience, and build courageous, purpose-driven teams.

Early Leadership The Apache Years

Shannon’s leadership journey began in uniform, where she faced the rigor, discipline, and uncertainty of flying the Apache helicopter. She learned firsthand that grit isn’t just persistence, it’s grounded in clarity of purpose.

Her military experience taught her how to:

Lead with decisiveness under pressure

In the cockpit, hesitation wasn’t an option. Shannon learned to absorb information quickly and act with confidence under pressure, shaping her leadership style and guiding how she teaches others.

Build high-performing teams in extreme conditions

Flying the Apache required trust and coordination. Shannon developed teams that communicate, support each other, and stay aligned in unpredictable conditions. She helps leaders build resilient, cohesive teams.

Navigate complex challenges with courage and strategy

In combat zones or dynamic missions, Shannon learned to simplify complexity and stay focused. She teaches leaders to tackle challenges with strategic thinking and confidence.

From the Military to Corporate Leadership

After her military service, Shannon transitioned to corporate leadership at Microsoft, where she led teams through innovation, change, and high-stakes projects.

She then founded The Grit Institute to bring research-backed frameworks and real-world lessons from the military and corporate world to leaders across industries.

Her Leadership Philosophy

Shannon believes that grit is grounded and holistic. True leadership emerges when purpose guides action. At The Grit Institute, she equips leaders to:

  • Align their actions with meaningful purpose

  • Build resilient teams that thrive under pressure

  • Lead with authenticity, courage, and clarity

What Grounds Her Life

Shannon lives with her husband, two boys and a menagerie of pets in the rural urban interface of Washington State, where she finds balance, clarity, and inspiration in nature. She is passionate about:

  • Mentoring women leaders and veterans

  • Creating meaningful learning experiences for leaders

  • Writing and storytelling as a tool for reflection and growth
